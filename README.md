1. Introduction
SMS Bulk Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using SMS Bulk Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect
To provide bulk SMS messaging functionality, SMS Bulk Pro collects and processes the following information:

A. User-Provided Data
Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and credentials provided during account setup.

Contacts & Recipient Data: Phone numbers and contact names imported or selected to receive bulk SMS messages.

Message Content & Templates: Text content, message templates, and transmission logs created within the app.

B. Automatically Collected Data
Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network information.

Log & Diagnostic Data: App usage statistics, delivery logs, error reports, and performance metrics.

C. App Permissions
SMS Bulk Pro requests specific permissions necessary for core operation:

SMS Permissions (SEND_SMS, READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS): Required to process, send, and log text messages.

Contacts Permission (READ_CONTACTS): Required to allow selection of message recipients from your address book.

Storage Permission: Required to import/export contact list files (e.g., CSV) and save local log reports.

3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Executing and tracking requested bulk SMS campaigns.

Storing recipient lists, message history, and templates locally or securely on your account.

Monitoring app performance, preventing fraud, and resolving technical issues.

Ensuring compliance with applicable telecommunications regulations and anti-spam laws (e.g., TCPA, CAN-SPAM).

4. Security & SSL Encryption
We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures:

Data in Transit: All communication between SMS Bulk Pro, backend servers, and third-party SMS gateways is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS) / Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) protocols (HTTPS).

Data at Rest: Sensitive credentials and stored message logs are encrypted using standard encryption algorithms.

5. Third-Party Services
SMS Bulk Pro may integrate with third-party SMS gateways, payment providers, or analytics services (such as Twilio, Firebase, or Stripe). These third parties process data strictly on our behalf to deliver app services and are restricted from using it for independent purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact lists or personal data to third-party advertisers.

6. Data Retention & User Control
Data Ownership: You retain full ownership of your contact lists and message history.

In-App Management: You can modify or delete saved contacts, templates, and message logs directly within the app at any time.

7. Children's Privacy
SMS Bulk Pro is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or 16 depending on local jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
